Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 22, 2026. This statement describes how the current NEXUS RNA BioSolutions research website is designed to handle information. The site is research-stage software; do not submit patient-identifiable, protected health or regulated confidential information.

Local research tools

Most basic sequence and analysis calculations run locally in your browser. Project data may be saved in browser storage on your device. That storage can remain accessible to others using the same browser profile and may be deleted when browser data are cleared. Exported files are managed by your device.

Optional accounts and cloud projects

When configured by the site operator, account access uses Supabase authentication. If you choose to sign in and manually upload a project to a configured cloud workspace, the account identifier and selected project data are sent to the cloud provider for account-specific storage. Cloud sync is not automatic. Configuration-dependent features may currently be disabled; see the sign-in page for status. Do not assume clinical-grade or regulated-data storage.

Contact requests and hosting

Scientific service inquiries may use an email-composition link that transfers your drafted content to your own email application. If you send it, your email provider and the recipient handle the message. Website hosting and infrastructure providers may process connection information and routine security or access logs. This site does not claim that it can erase third-party records.

External scientific resources

Links to resources such as NCBI, AlphaFold DB and third-party tools open external sites subject to their own privacy policies. Do not submit sensitive sequences to an external provider without reviewing its terms and your confidentiality obligations.

Security and controls

Use nonconfidential demonstration sequences until cloud configurations, access policies, backups and operational security have been independently reviewed. Cloud projects have deletion controls when activated; local browser data may be cleared through your browser. For access and privacy inquiries, use the scientific-services contact workflow on the NEXUS website.

Policy updates

This statement may be revised when account, analytics, payment, or data-processing capabilities change. Review this page before submitting research data.
