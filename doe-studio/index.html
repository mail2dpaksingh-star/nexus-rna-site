2 · DOE planning checklist

  • Define experimental unit: an independent formulation batch, RNA synthesis, biological donor or culture—not technical repeat wells alone.
  • Set response and criteria first: state measurement units, method, direction of improvement and acceptable range.
  • Protect randomization: consider day, instrument, operator, cell passage, and raw-material lots as possible blocks or covariates.
  • Control confounding: fractional factorials alias effects; consider fold-over or follow-up confirmation before mechanistic conclusions.
  • Check curvature: factorials with center points can screen curvature, but a quadratic model requires a suitable response-surface design.
  • Validate predictions: replicate the proposed optimum and compare to a predefined control before making claims.
