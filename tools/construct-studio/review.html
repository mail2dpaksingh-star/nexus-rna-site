3 · Scientific acceptance boundary

Blocking issues mean the supplied architecture is internally inconsistent or incomplete. Review findings mean experimental interpretation or documentation is required. A report with zero blocking issues does not mean the construct is safe, effective, manufacturable or analytically qualified.

For a project-level workflow, pair this design review with independently measured RNA identity/integrity, capping, poly(A) distribution, cell expression, localization, immune assay results and version-controlled construct records. See Project Hub.
