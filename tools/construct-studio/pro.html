Promoter engineering worksheet

A DNA-template comparison tool, intentionally separate from the mature RNA sequence.

One-based substitutions only. The tool checks base identity and builds a hypothetical comparison variant; no strength optimization.

Choose the correct promoter system

T7 / SP6 / T3 · IVTDNA-template recognition sites for phage RNA polymerases. Verify transcript-start position, sequence context and polymerase-specific manufacturing requirements.
CMV / EF1α / CAG / PGK · cellularDNA-vector promoter contexts, not generic add-ons for purified mRNA delivered to cells. Motif analysis cannot establish expression level.
U6 · small RNARNA polymerase III context commonly used for small RNA transcription, not interchangeable with mammalian protein-coding RNA polymerase II promoters.
A promoter generally remains on DNA and is not incorporated into the mature mRNA. For a separately delivered, in-vitro-transcribed mRNA product, the promoter affects the manufacturing template, while CDS/UTRs affect the RNA product.
