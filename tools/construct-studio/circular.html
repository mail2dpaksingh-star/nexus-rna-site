Design the circular RNA hypothesis

Scientific interpretation

Not a linear mRNA

Circular RNA generally lacks a free 5′ cap and terminal poly(A) tail. mRNA capping and mammalian Kozak optimization cannot simply be carried over as equivalent design controls.

Internal initiation

Some coding circRNAs use internal ribosome entry or m6A-related initiation. This tool checks only the sequence you provide, not its activity.

Verification

Confirm sequence across the closed junction, circularity relative to linear contamination, translation product identity, innate sensing and relevant functional expression.
