3 · Assay qualification worksheet

Enter only limits that have been prospectively specified in a controlled method or study plan. Missing criteria remain “Not assessed.”

Validation gate: Precision, bias/recovery, specificity, linearity, reportable range, robustness, sample stability and interference must be qualified for the intended analytical purpose. This worksheet checks only the numerical limits provided here; a clean report is not proof of analytical method validation.

Method qualification evidence register

References below record where supporting validation evidence may be found; entering them does not mean the assay is qualified.

Coverage by assay family

  • qPCR: Cq/Ct table import; amplification/melt curves and ΔΔCq not reconstructed from an export.
  • ddPCR: imported copies/µL or concentration; event-to-copy calculation requires separate validated droplet volume/threshold inputs.
  • LC–MS: peak areas/abundances; no cap percentage without peak identity, response calibration and a suitable quantitative method.
  • Bioanalyzer: peak and region statistics; does not parse electropherogram binaries or infer RNA integrity from arbitrary areas.
  • Plate readers: RFU/RLU/OD exports; blank and calibration controls must be independently verified.
