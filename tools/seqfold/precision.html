RNA hairpin and secondary-structure review

Up to 3,000 nt · windowed above 220

Ready.

Not ViennaRNA RNAfold. For sequences longer than 220 nt, independent 200-nt windows are used and long-range cross-window interactions are not evaluated. The model scores hairpins, stacked base pairs and limited internal loops only; no multiloop thermodynamics, dangling ends, complete experimental parameter tables, ensemble or base-pair probabilities. NEXUS does not calculate ΔG (kcal/mol); supply a thermodynamic result from a named external program. Do not compare scores across RNAs as binding affinity or stability.
