Restriction site map

Exact recognition-site search for selected enzymes on a linear sequence. Positions are 1-based and cut coordinates are between bases; methylation, partial digests and circular templates are not modeled.

Pairwise nucleotide alignment

Needleman–Wunsch global or Smith–Waterman local alignment with a constant gap penalty. For quick comparisons up to 700 nucleotides per sequence; not equivalent to a genome alignment pipeline.

RNA secondary-structure explorer

Computes a pseudoknot-free, maximum-pair structure using Nussinov dynamic programming. Supports AU, GC and optional GU wobble pairing. This does not calculate free energy or ViennaRNA RNAfold thermodynamics.

Select a region up to 350 nucleotides, then predict.
The RNA pairing diagram appears here.
Interpretation limit: A maximum-pair structure is an educational hypothesis, not a folding-energy, base-pair-probability, or experimental structure result. Do not use this alone for vaccine design, RNA stability decisions, or clinical applications. For validated thermodynamic analysis, use ViennaRNA RNAfold.
