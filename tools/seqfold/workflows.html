RNA Structure Workbench

A single, organized starting point for sequence preparation, RNA secondary-structure exploration, free-energy analysis, ensembles, references and benchmarking. Existing scientific tools remain available; their workflows are grouped below rather than listed repeatedly.

Consolidated navigation, not a new validated folding engine. ThermoFold remains a research-stage prototype; predictions and numerical results should be interpreted alongside references and appropriate experimental evidence. Sequences are not automatically transferred between separate tools.
