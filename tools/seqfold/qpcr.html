qPCR & ddPCR Primer / Probe Designer

Generate research-stage PCR primer pairs and internal hydrolysis-probe candidates from DNA/cDNA, inspect predicted sequence metrics and potential secondary-structure risks, and export your assay designs. Computation stays on your device.

1 · Target sequence and assay preferences

Use a target DNA or cDNA region, with optional FASTA header. One record per run; A/C/G/T and unresolved N positions are supported. Oligos will not overlap N positions.

Enter your template, select assay settings, and run the designer.

Research use only: This is an original heuristic screening engine based on SeqFold's existing unmodified DNA Tm estimate. It is not Primer3, Primer-BLAST, a validated diagnostic assay, or a substitute for in vitro efficiency/specificity tests. It cannot predict modified-probe Tm, isoform specificity, genomic DNA exclusion, or clinical diagnostic performance.
