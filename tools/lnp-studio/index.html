Review and QC warnings

    Targeted delivery is not predicted from ligand percentage, charge, FRR or particle size. Surface accessibility, ligand affinity, circulation, tissue penetration, endosomal escape and biodistribution require empirical measurement. Nominal N:P is not effective protonation.

    Targeted LNP qualification considerations

    Use a risk-based, evidence-supported review rather than a computer-generated targeting score.

    Ligand and receptor biology

    Tissue specificity · receptor accessibility · on-target/off-tissue expression · binding/internalization · competition with native ligands

    Physicochemical profile

    DLS/PDI · particle concentration · encapsulation efficiency · RNA integrity · free ligand/free RNA · surface charge · stability

    Cellular potency

    Cell type, dose/viability, RNA expression, functional activity, controls and endosomal escape evidence

    Safety and manufacturing

    Innate immune activation · lipid/RNA impurities · batch comparability · sterility/endotoxin as appropriate · FTO
