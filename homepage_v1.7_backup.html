Oncology immunogenomics · Research workflow

Personalized cancer vaccine design.

Connect tumor genomic evidence, HLA context, computational neoantigen prioritization, and mRNA construct research. NEXUS provides a framework for exploring evidence and accessing established public resources—not an automated clinical vaccine-design service.

  1. 01 / GENOMICS

    Interpret tumor variation

    Review tumor-versus-normal somatic variants, relevant fusions, and expression evidence.

  2. 02 / IMMUNOGENETICS

    Consider HLA context

    Use appropriately obtained individual HLA class I and II types for peptide presentation analyses.

  3. 03 / NEOANTIGENS

    Prioritize candidates

    Review predicted presentation, mutation support, expression, clonality, and normal-tissue relevance.

  4. 04 / RNA RESEARCH

    Explore construct concepts

    Assess antigen representation, RNA design hypotheses, delivery, and quality attributes.

  5. 05 / VALIDATION

    Evaluate experimental evidence

    Plan confirmation of peptide presentation, immunogenicity, specificity, and functional responses.
