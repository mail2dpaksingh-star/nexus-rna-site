Molecular & Genomic Analysis

Research bioinformatics, molecular assays and translational biomarker strategy.

Proposed scientific services

  1. NGS and RNA-seq analysis planningData-analysis strategies, QC criteria, reproducible pipelines and interpretation plans.
  2. Oncology biomarker discoveryEvidence reviews and research-stage mutation or expression-marker prioritization.

SERVICE CATEGORY 02

RNA Design & Engineering

Construct engineering and comparative sequence design for research-stage RNA platforms.

Proposed scientific services

  1. Molecular RNA Therapeutics EngineeringMulti-objective mRNA design, RNA silencing and splice-modulation research, programmable editing feasibility, circRNA/saRNA comparisons, and RNA–LNP co-design. Explore molecular RNA drug services →
  2. mRNA construct strategyResearch coding-sequence architecture, expression considerations and design alternatives.
  3. UTR, codon and poly(A) optimizationComputational comparisons and experiments to test sequence-design hypotheses.
  4. saRNA and circular RNA architecturesExploratory construct architecture and research feasibility assessment.
  5. Plasmid design and cloning supportConstruct maps, annotated features, sequence regions and cloning design checks.
  6. RNA folding and structure reviewPredicted RNA secondary structures and uncertainty-aware structural comparisons.
  7. Construct quality and experimental evidence reviewSequence QC, analytical endpoints, control strategy and evidence integration.

Typical deliverables: Annotated sequences and plasmid maps, alternative construct comparisons and an experimental validation plan.

Scope note: Predicted folding and construct rankings are research outputs, not clinically or experimentally validated performance claims.
