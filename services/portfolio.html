Delivery & Therapeutic Development

RNA delivery strategy, formulation screening, analytical development and translation planning.

  1. LNP formulation research planningCandidate and screening choices across ionizable lipid headgroup/linker degradability, PEG-lipid content and anchor type, sterol identity, optional SORT lipid class, and formulation QC endpoints. Design assumptions are documented for experimental comparison, not organ-targeting prediction.
  2. Targeted RNA delivery conceptsExploratory delivery and targeting hypotheses with biological evaluation plans.
  3. Formulation DoE and optimizationMixture and process DoE matrices for experiment prioritization.
  4. RNA / LNP analytical method planningParticle, encapsulation, integrity and stability assay plans and criteria.
  5. Expression and immunology study planningCell-based expression, immune-response and functional endpoint study designs.
  6. Preclinical research and partner coordinationRoute-of-administration feasibility planning: intravenous (IV), intramuscular (IM), subcutaneous (SC), intradermal (ID), intranasal or inhaled, and local routes such as intratumoral/intrathecal where scientifically appropriate. Compare route-specific biodistribution, exposure, delivery barriers, tolerability and analytical endpoints; coordinate study design, technical milestones and qualified laboratory partners. These are research options, not validated clinical routes or dosing recommendations.
  7. Bulk-Scale RNA Production & Purification PlatformModular IVT process-development, controlled reactor engineering, TFF/chromatography purification strategies, and RNA analytical QC. Physical bulk RNA production requires a commissioned facility or qualified partner capacity. Explore the RNA production platform →

Typical deliverables: Formulation research design, DoE plan, analytical testing strategy and partner-ready technical dossier.

Scope note: Physical LNP formulation, GMP production and laboratory testing require suitable facilities or contracted partners.
