Client-facing molecular RNA services

Modular, computational-first services that can be scoped individually or combined into a multi-disciplinary RNA program.

01 / MOLECULAR DESIGN

AI-assisted mRNA sequence engineering

Compare candidate coding regions, 5′/3′ UTRs, codon usage, poly(A), cap and modified-nucleotide strategies. Document predicted stability, expression trade-offs and RNA structural features rather than claiming guaranteed potency.

Computational research scope
02 / GENE MODULATION

siRNA, ASO & splice-modulation strategy

Target-transcript selection, isoform analysis, candidate region evaluation, sequence specificity, potential off-target binding and rational experimental readouts for RNA knockdown or splice switching.

Design / feasibility scope
03 / PRECISION EDITING

Programmable RNA editing feasibility

Research-stage guide design concepts for ADAR-mediated transcript editing, targetability review, anticipated bystander-editing risks and orthogonal validation plans. No claim of a validated editing engine.

Exploratory / partner-dependent
04 / ADVANCED ARCHITECTURES

circRNA and self-amplifying RNA

Evaluate linear mRNA versus circular RNA and saRNA architecture, expression duration, topology, payload complexity, purification considerations and assay requirements.

Research-stage platform comparison
05 / PRECISION ONCOLOGY

RNA cancer immunotherapy design

Candidate tumor antigen and neoantigen evidence review, HLA-restricted epitope prioritization strategy, construct architecture and immune-monitoring endpoints for research-stage therapeutic vaccine programs.

Predictions require biological testing
06 / DELIVERY ENGINEERING

RNA–LNP molecular co-design

Connect RNA payload properties with LNP formulation hypotheses, cell-type and organ-targeting objectives, endosomal escape considerations, and the resulting analytical and preclinical study plan.

Computational / partner-supported

What the client receives

  • Target and molecular-mechanism dossier with cited evidence
  • Annotated RNA constructs or candidate design alternatives, as appropriate
  • Sequence/structure and off-target risk comparisons with assumptions and limitations
  • Multi-criteria candidate decision matrix and design rationale
  • Assay design, analytical QC, research-study and delivery plans
  • Reproducible research files and technology-transfer documentation where scoped
  • Project-specific novelty and patent freedom-to-operate assessment gate
Availability: Research consulting, computational analysis and project design are the starting offerings. Experimental synthesis, formulation, biological testing, scaled production and regulated activities require confirmed facilities, qualified partners and a separately agreed scope. Research use only; not a clinical diagnostic or therapeutic efficacy claim.
