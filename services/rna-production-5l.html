What NEXUS can support

IVT process design & scale-up

Reactor selection, mixing and thermal-control strategy, reagent-feed architecture and process mapping for research-stage mRNA.

Reactor system engineering

Conceptual 8–10 L vessel configuration sized for a 5 L working reaction, single-use fluid path and instrumentation specification.

Upstream-to-downstream integration

Template handling, co-transcriptional capping options, residual-template removal, closed transfer and recovery planning.

Purification process development

Tangential flow filtration, buffer exchange, RNA capture and impurity-polishing strategies suited to the construct and research objective.

RNA analytical characterization

Research methods for RNA quantity, integrity, residual DNA, dsRNA and applicable cap/poly(A) assessments.

Technology transfer & partner support

Equipment RFQs, batch records, process-flow documentation, technical packages and qualified external laboratory coordination.
