RESEARCH LANDSCAPE

Approaches under investigation in oncology

NEXUS maps application questions to relevant research tools. Clinical readiness differs by approach, and software outputs require independent biological validation.

Investigational clinical research

Personalized neoantigen vaccines

Prioritize candidate altered peptides using independently reviewed tumor variation, expression and HLA evidence, then compare RNA construct and antigen-expression hypotheses.

Public neoantigen predictions do not prove antigen presentation or patient response.

Preclinical and clinical investigation

Immune microenvironment modulation

Explore mRNA-encoded cytokines, immune ligands and localized immunomodulatory expression as research concepts; evaluate functional readouts and off-target considerations.

Systemic delivery and tumor penetration are unresolved across many tumor settings.

Preclinical research

mRNA-encoded therapeutic proteins

Organize antibody, engager or antigen-expression constructs and compare analytical identity, protein expression, and functional assay evidence.

Protein expression is not evidence of clinical efficacy.

INTERPRETATION & QUALITY

Measure the right outcomes, not just proxy signals.

Targeting and expression need multiple independent forms of evidence; no single readout proves clinical effectiveness.

Measurements to distinguish

  • Mutation identified vs neoantigen independently prioritized
  • RNA delivery vs verified antigen expression
  • Antigen expression vs antigen presentation
  • Immune activation vs antigen-specific activity

Known constraints

  • HLA variation and tumor heterogeneity
  • Delivery to desired tumor or immune-cell populations
  • Endosomal escape and off-target distribution
  • RNA integrity, immunogenicity and repeat dosing
