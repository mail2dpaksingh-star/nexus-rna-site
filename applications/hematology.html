RESEARCH LANDSCAPE

Research opportunities across hematologic systems

Hematology programs require cell- and compartment-specific evidence. Ex vivo engineered cellular therapies are clinically established for certain indications, whereas in vivo RNA–LNP programming remains investigational.

Investigational

In vivo CAR-T and CAR-NK programming

Investigate transient RNA-encoded receptor expression and targeted immune-cell delivery in disease-relevant research systems.

Expression of a CAR alone does not establish killing efficacy or safe cell specificity.

Early translational / preclinical

HSPC and marrow-directed delivery

Plan research around RNA cargo delivery to hematopoietic stem/progenitor populations, with attention to self-renewal, lineage output and off-target marrow exposure.

In vivo HSPC targeting remains challenging and is not a validated NEXUS prediction.

Disease-relevant research

Leukemia, lymphoma & myeloma models

Use publicly accessible cancer data and cell-function endpoints to study constructs, delivery strategies and analytical evidence.

Normal lymphocyte and marrow compartments must be evaluated alongside malignant targets.

INTERPRETATION & QUALITY

Measure the right outcomes, not just proxy signals.

Targeting and expression need multiple independent forms of evidence; no single readout proves clinical effectiveness.

Distinguish these endpoints

  • Total LNP uptake vs productive RNA delivery
  • CAR surface expression vs antigen-specific killing
  • Short-term transfection vs repeat-dose feasibility
  • HSPC delivery vs retained long-term stem-cell function

Scientific barriers

  • Liver, spleen and marrow biodistribution
  • Resting T/NK-cell transfection and cell viability
  • Off-target normal immune and stem-cell populations
  • Repeat dosing, innate immune reactions and batch consistency
