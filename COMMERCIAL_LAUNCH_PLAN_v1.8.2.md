# NEXUS v1.8.2 — commercial MVP plan

## Positioning
NEXUS RNA BioSolutions: research software and scientific development support across RNA engineering, LNP formulation strategy, analytical QC and DBTL. Research-stage; not a GMP release platform.

## Revenue order
1. Scientific service pilots first: design review ($1.5k–3k), LNP/DOE feasibility ($4k–8k), integrated research pilot ($10k–25k+). Proposed quotations only.
2. Design partners: 3–5 laboratories to validate data formats, measured time savings and software gaps with synthetic/nonconfidential test files.
3. SaaS research subscriptions only after customer validation, account security, access controls, operational support and payment infrastructure.

## Feature boundaries
**Keep free**: single-sequence RNA/DNA analysis, translation, basic CDS/UTR proposals, basic primers/qPCR/ddPCR design, single-run assay calculations, public AlphaFold DB links, guest-local project files.
**Research Pro**: secure saved workspace, project templates and version snapshots, batch candidate comparison, repeated instrument-export analysis, richer DOE models/report templates, validated data processing at specified formats.
**Laboratory Team**: organization workspace, role-based access, shared projects, data provenance, sample/batch traceability, instrument-mapping templates, change reviews and team reports.
**Enterprise**: private deployment, SSO, tailored integrations and method qualification services. Compliance is **not included by default**.

## 90-day validation gates
0–30 days: confirm business entity/contracts, nonconfidential demo, contact channel, three scoped offers, conduct ten target interviews.
31–60 days: aim for first paid service pilot; qualify 3 representative vendor CSV exports; measure delivery time and customer value.
61–90 days: target 2 more projects and 3 design partners; prioritize features based on retained usage and willingness to pay, not added feature count.

## Go/no-go before paid checkout
- Operational authentication and per-customer authorization; separate staging/production.
- Encrypted, recoverable server storage and deletion/export capability.
- Access and usage logging, dependency/license review, independent penetration/security review.
- Pricing terms, refunds, privacy policy, incident response and support commitments.
- Reproducible validation reference datasets for any claim of analytical accuracy.
