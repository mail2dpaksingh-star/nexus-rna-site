          mRNA-Based NK Cell Engineering

          Use the mRNA-Based NK Cell Engineering option above to compare transient CAR-NK expression, non-CAR receptor or trafficking engineering, and immune-support payload hypotheses. The interactive output adapts its conceptual RNA architecture, NK-specific functional evidence priorities, safety flags and downloadable research plan.

          Cell engineering & expression

          Consider the NK-cell source, identity and viability, RNA integrity and innate sensing, CAR or payload surface expression (when applicable), and time-dependent expression decay.

          Functional evidence & safety

          Prioritize antigen-positive and antigen-negative specificity controls where relevant, cytotoxicity, CD107a degranulation, cytokine responses, donor variability, and healthy-cell off-target risk.
